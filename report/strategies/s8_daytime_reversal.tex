\subsection{S8: overnight returns and daytime reversals}\label{sec:s8}

\paragraph{Economic intuition and literature.}
Overnight noise-trader demand may push prices up overnight, and intraday arbitrageurs may push back. \citet{akbas2022} relate the monthly frequency of negative daytime reversals (positive overnight return followed by a negative intraday return) to future returns. We pre-registered a positive sign (high frequency, higher next-month return), following that paper. The citation is to be verified against the source.

\paragraph{Specification.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Signal:} NR is the fraction of the last 21 sessions with an overnight return above 0 and an intraday return below 0, computed from auction prints,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{NR}_{i} = \frac{1}{21}\sum_{s=1}^{21} \mathbf{1}\{r^{\mathrm{ovn}}_{i,s} > 0,\; r^{\mathrm{intra}}_{i,s} < 0\}.
\end{equation}
\item \emph{Universe:} names with at least 15 valid sessions.
\item \emph{Portfolio:} at the month-end close, go long the top third and short the bottom third by NR. The book is dollar neutral and held for one month.
\item \emph{Execution tier:} T1 closes.
\item \emph{Status:} confirmatory but partially informed, because S8 is closest to wave-2 H3.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Implementation.}
The engine is \file{wave3/cpp/s_calendar.cpp}. Split-adjusted share carry was fixed before the run. With 12--13 eligible names per month, each tercile holds four names, so the ranking is coarse.

\paragraph{Out-of-sample testing and results.}
Development annual excess return is $-1.8\%$ (Sharpe $-0.13$, NW $t = -0.20$) and the 2024 result is $+19.6\%$ (Sharpe 1.69) (Table~\ref{tab:s8}). The primary is rejected by the development criterion of the kill rule, so the 2024 result does not rescue it.
