\subsection{S4: pre-announcement option flow}\label{sec:s4}

\paragraph{Economic intuition and literature.}
Informed trading may concentrate in options before scheduled news \citep{roll2010,johnsonso2012}. S4 asks whether the direction of option flow in the week before earnings predicts the announcement return.

\paragraph{Specification.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Signal:} the delta-weighted flow ratio of \eqref{eq:flow}, with numerator and denominator summed separately over sessions $X-5,\dots,X-1$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Flow}^{\mathrm{pre}} = \frac{\sum_{s=X-5}^{X-1} \mathrm{num}_s}{\sum_{s=X-5}^{X-1} \mathrm{den}_s}.
\end{equation}
\item \emph{Rule:} buy or sell at the close of $X$ in the direction of $\mathrm{sign}(\mathrm{Flow}^{\mathrm{pre}})$. Exit at the first opening cross after the announcement, or at that day's close if the cross is missing. Beta-hedge with SPY.
\item \emph{Portfolio:} 10\% of equity per event.
\item \emph{Status:} exploratory, because the signal is derived from H4.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Implementation.}
The engine is \file{wave3/cpp/s_events.cpp}. Because S4 is exploratory, it runs on the full 2022--2024 sample and cannot be confirmed here.

\paragraph{Out-of-sample testing and results.}
The result is $+0.41\%$ per event in development ($t = 0.38$) and $+0.07\%$ in 2024 (Table~\ref{tab:s4}). We detect no effect at this sample size.
