\subsection{H2: overnight versus intraday option premium}\label{sec:h2}

\paragraph{Economic intuition and literature.}
\citet{muravyevni2020} find that delta-hedged option returns are strongly negative overnight. \citet{jones2018} report lower option returns over non-trading periods. Option prices seem to spread expected variance evenly over calendar time, while most variance arrives during the session, so short sellers of overnight variance should be paid.

\paragraph{Specification.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Signal:} unconditional, every session pair (close of $t$ to open of $t+1$). A secondary restricts to weekends.
\item \emph{Portfolio:} short one ATM SPY or QQQ straddle (strike nearest $F$, first expiry with DTE $\ge 7$), delta-hedged with shares at entry using the Black--Scholes delta from IV inverted at the mid. The size $n$ limits the straddle's gamma loss on a 3\% gap to 0.5\% of equity,
\begin{equation}
n = \left\lfloor \frac{0.005\,E}{\tfrac{1}{2}\,\Gamma\,(0.03\,S)^{2} \times 100} \right\rfloor ,
\end{equation}
computed for SPY and QQQ independently.
\item \emph{Execution tier:} option entry at 15:58:00 and exit at 09:33:00 the next session (both T2). The stock hedge is T2 against the equity NBBO at the same times.
\item \emph{Risk limits:} gap risk is bounded by the sizing rule, not by construction. We report the worst nights.
\item \emph{Contrast:} the same trade held intraday, 09:33 to 15:58.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Implementation.}
The engine is \file{wave2/cpp/h2_overnight.cpp}. Options are T2, and the stock hedge is T2 against the equity NBBO, which exists exactly in the open and close windows. The pre-registered contract rule filled 10 nights, so the original primary is untestable. Amendment A1 (made only because of measured fill availability) chooses among contracts with observed quotes and allows a 120 s exit chase. The P\&L decomposition identities hold to within \$0.005.

\paragraph{Out-of-sample testing and results.}
The A1 overnight trade lost 1.25\% of premium per night in development (date-clustered $t = -4.0$). Table~\ref{tab:h2} decomposes the result (\figref{fig:h2}{fig06_h2_decomposition.png}). In this sample the option-value decline appears mostly during the session, which is the opposite of \citet{muravyevni2020}. The 09:33 opening quote may carry an opening-IV premium. The spread alone is about four times the overnight premium. Annual excess return is $-0.8\%$ (Sharpe $-1.39$, NW $t=-2.22$) in development and $+0.9\%$ (Sharpe 2.44) in the holdout. The decision is reject.
