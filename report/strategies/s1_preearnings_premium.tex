\subsection{S1: pre-earnings announcement premium}\label{sec:s1}

\paragraph{Economic intuition and literature.}
Stocks earn higher returns in the days before scheduled earnings. This is consistent with compensation for announcement risk and with attention-driven buying pressure \citep{frazzini2007,barber2013}.

\paragraph{Specification.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Signal:} the earnings calendar, under the assumption that dates are known at least 7 sessions ahead. $X$ is the last session before the announcement (as in H5).
\item \emph{Rule:} buy at the close of $X-5$ and sell at the close of $X$, before the announcement.
\item \emph{Hedge:} short SPY at the same auction, with the ratio set to the 60-session OLS beta of daily close-to-close returns ending the day before entry, capped to $[0.5, 2]$.
\item \emph{Portfolio:} 10\% of equity per event, with overlapping events allowed.
\item \emph{Execution tier:} T1 closing prices.
\item \emph{Secondaries:} unhedged, and a 10-session window.
\item \emph{Test:} mean hedged event return (clustered by date) and the account Sharpe ratio.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Implementation.}
The engine is \file{wave3/cpp/s_events.cpp}, with official-close fills and a daily marked book. The official close equals the closing-cross print on 100\% of tested days. The wave-3 stock book reconciles exactly to its ledger. Confirmatory split: development 2022-01-03 to 2023-12-29, test year 2024.

\paragraph{Out-of-sample testing and results.}
The development hedged event return is $+0.46\%$ (105 events, clustered $t = 1.47$), with annual excess return $+2.4\%$ (NW $t = 1.08$). The 2024 test is $+0.06\%$ per event (60 events, $t = 0.02$) and $+0.4\%$ annualized (Table~\ref{tab:s1}). Unhedged, development is $+1.10\%$ per event ($t = 2.28$), mostly market exposure, and the 2024 test is about 0 (\figref{fig:s1events}{fig13_w3_events.png}). S1 survives the kill rule narrowly, but the premium did not appear in 2024. The result is not significant.
