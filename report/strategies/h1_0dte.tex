\subsection{H1: 0DTE SPXW premium held to settlement}\label{sec:h1}

\paragraph{Economic intuition and literature.}
Same-day index options may embed a variance premium, because demand for same-day convexity from retail traders and hedgers can exceed the risk that dealers are compensated for \citep{bandi2023,beckmeyer2023,vilkov2023}. Holding to cash settlement pays the spread once and collects the premium without a second spread.

\paragraph{Specification.}
The primary signal is unconditional: every day with a 0DTE SPXW expiry and valid quotes. The forward $F_\tau$ is the parity forward of \eqref{eq:forward}, built from each contract's last quote in $[\tau - 120\,\mathrm{s}, \tau)$.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Portfolio:} short iron fly. Sell the call and put at $K^{*}$ and buy the wings at $K^{*} \pm w$, where $w$ is the listed strike nearest $1.5 \times$ the straddle mid (minimum 5 points). One trade per day.
\item \emph{Sizing and risk:} contracts $n$ are sized so that the maximum loss satisfies
\begin{equation}
(w - \text{credit}) \times 100 \times n \;\le\; 0.005\,E,
\end{equation}
where $E$ is account equity. Risk is defined by construction. We report the worst day, ES97.5 and the share of P\&L from the best 5 days.
\item \emph{Primary $\tau$:} 12:00 ET. Secondaries are 10:00, 14:00 and 15:00, and a short iron condor (shorts at $K^{*} \pm 1 \times$ the straddle, wings at $K^{*} \pm 2 \times$ the straddle).
\item \emph{Secondary signal:} a filter that trades only when the implied move (ATM straddle mid divided by $F$) exceeds the trailing-20-session median realized move.
\item \emph{Execution tier:} T2 entry (sell at the bid, buy at the ask) and T0 exit at \texttt{sp500\_cash\_level}.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Implementation.}
The engine is \file{wave2/cpp/h1_0dte.cpp}. Fills are T2 on all four legs, and all legs must fill. Four-leg fills succeeded on about 80\% of days, so H1 is the one wave-2 option design with adequate fill availability and no amendment. The 0DTE settlement close matches the intraday parity forward to within about 2 bp on average.

\paragraph{Out-of-sample testing and results.}
The development mean return on risk is $-4.0\%$ per trade (date-clustered $t = -1.22$), with annual excess return $-3.1\%$ and Sharpe $-0.79$ (Table~\ref{tab:h1}). The holdout is $+3.0\%$ (confidence interval $-9\%$ to $+16\%$). Later entries are closer to zero, and the condor is about 0. None of the 15 variants is significant after Romano--Wolf correction (\figref{fig:h1}{fig05_h1_entry_latency.png}). The decision is reject.
