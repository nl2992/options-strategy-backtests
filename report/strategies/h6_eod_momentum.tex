\subsection{H6: end-of-day momentum}\label{sec:h6}

\paragraph{Economic intuition and literature.}
Negative-gamma dealer hedging and late-informed trading may push the day's move further into the close \citep{gaohan2018,baltussen2021}.

\paragraph{Specification.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Signal:} the return from the prior close to 15:55,
\begin{equation}
r = \frac{\mathrm{mid}(15{:}55)}{\mathrm{close}_{t-1}} - 1,
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{close}_{t-1}$ is the official closing auction print. The primary position is $\mathrm{sign}(r)$ every day. The secondary trades only on days when the prior-day dealer gamma exposure is negative,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{GEX} = \sum_{\text{calls}} \Gamma \cdot \mathrm{OI} \cdot 100 \cdot S^{2} \cdot 0.01 \;-\; \sum_{\text{puts}} \Gamma \cdot \mathrm{OI} \cdot 100 \cdot S^{2} \cdot 0.01,
\end{equation}
under the labelled assumption that dealers are long calls and short puts.
\item \emph{Portfolio:} 50\% of equity in SPY and 50\% in QQQ, each signed independently.
\item \emph{Execution tier:} entry at 15:55 plus latency against the equity NBBO (T2, size at most the displayed size) and exit at the closing auction print (T1).
\item \emph{Evaluation:} daily net return, Newey--West $t$, hit rate, and a placebo with randomized signs (1,000 draws).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Implementation.}
The engine is \file{wave2/cpp/h6_eodmom.cpp}. Amendment A2 responds to data availability: the equity quote window opens at 15:55:00, so the signal uses the mid of the first quote at 15:55:00.5, which is observable at that time. A missing equity-quote window blanked H6 in an early mechanics check, and the bug was fixed before any out-of-sample run. Displayed-size caps filled about 47\% of the target notional.

\paragraph{Out-of-sample testing and results.}
The development Sharpe ratio is 0.91 ($t = 1.50$), at the 99.3rd percentile of 1,000 random-sign placebos. The holdout Sharpe ratio is $-1.37$, at the 24th percentile (\figref{fig:h6}{fig10_h6_placebo.png}). The decision is reject.
