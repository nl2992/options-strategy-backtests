\subsection{H3: overnight equity drift with auction fills}\label{sec:h3}

\paragraph{Economic intuition and literature.}
A large share of the equity premium may accrue overnight, through a tug of war between overnight and intraday clienteles \citep{lou2019,cliff2008}. Auctions allow entry and exit at a single price without crossing a spread.

\paragraph{Specification.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Signal:} the primary is an always-on long overnight position. The secondary is overnight momentum: rank names on their trailing-21-session mean overnight return and hold only the top third.
\item \emph{Portfolio:} equal weight across 14 single names plus SPY and QQQ, 100\% gross, rebuilt daily. META and GOOGL are excluded because they lack auction coverage.
\item \emph{Execution tier:} T1. Buy at the closing cross and sell at the next opening cross, with fees on each side and participation at most 1\% of lagged cross size. A sensitivity crosses the spread at 15:59 and 09:31 instead of using auctions.
\item \emph{Contrast:} the intraday (open to close) leg.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Implementation.}
The engine is \file{wave2/cpp/h3_overnight_eq.cpp}. Fills are T1. Splits are handled with \texttt{share\_adj\_factor}, and ex-dividend nights are credited the dividend from the gap between total and price return. Auction crosses are validated against the official open and close.

\paragraph{Out-of-sample testing and results.}
Development overnight returns are $+4.8\%$ per year (CI $-12.8\%$ to $+22.2\%$; Sharpe 0.29, NW $t=0.50$), against $+15.3\%$ for the intraday leg, the reverse of the documented pattern (\figref{fig:h3}{fig07_h3_overnight_intraday.png}). The 2024H2 result ($+28.8\%$, Sharpe 2.44) coincides with a broad rally and does not survive removing the five best days. The decision is reject.
